name: Minigame scope
description: User-directed boundaries for the playable BedWars practice game
---
The user wants a sign-in-required, 2D 1v1 BedWars game against a bot with blue and orange teams. The Arena offers The Pit and Ranked beside each other, plus Shop; there is no Settings tab. The Pit uses normal difficulty, five map choices, iron only at one per 50 ms, blocks/swords/axes in its match shop, and D-tier free kits with C/B/A/S unlocks costing 20/30/40/50 coins. Four separate 10-coin crates unlock nonduplicate sword, kit, block, and axe skins; show the full skin catalog without fixed-height internal scrolling.

Ranked starts at 0 RP and awards exactly +25 for a win or −10 for a loss; RP may go below zero while the player remains Unranked. Every rank step is 100 RP: Unranked, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Emerald, then Nightmare at 600. Ranked has two home islands and one middle island with no prebuilt bridge, no kits or abilities, no starting blocks or axe, and an in-match shop limited to blocks and axes.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a simple, reliable, sign-in-required game and later added a separate ranked mode with exact point values and no-kit rules.

**How to apply:** Keep The Pit’s existing limits and apply the separate rules above only to Ranked. Do not add diamond or emerald resources, armor, ranged weapons, online multiplayer, or guest play without a new request.

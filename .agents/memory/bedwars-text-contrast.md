---
name: BedWars text contrast
description: Readability guidance for the BedWars red-and-black visual theme
---
Keep the red-and-black theme, but use light rose or neutral red-tinted body text against dark surfaces; reserve deeper red for accents and use semantic colors where they communicate status.

**Why:** A blanket dark-red text color became difficult to read against the app's dark backgrounds, and the user asked to fix the result.

**How to apply:** Preserve hierarchy and contrast when styling text. Do not apply one forced color to every element.
